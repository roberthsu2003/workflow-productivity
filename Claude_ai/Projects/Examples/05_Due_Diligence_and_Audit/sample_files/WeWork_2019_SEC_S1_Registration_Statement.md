# UNITED STATES SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION (SEC)
## FORM S-1 REGISTRATION STATEMENT - The We Company (WeWork)

**Filing Date**: August 14, 2019  
**Target IPO Valuation**: $47,000,000,000 USD ($47 Billion)

### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & BUSINESS MODEL

The We Company (WeWork) provides flexible workspace solutions globally. As of June 30, 2019, we had 528 locations across 111 cities in 29 countries, with total membership reaching 527,000.

#### Key Financial Highlights (as reported in S-1):
* **2018 Revenue**: $1,821,700,000 USD ($1.82 Billion)
* **2018 Net Loss**: $1,927,300,000 USD ($1.93 Billion)
* **2019 H1 Revenue**: $1,535,000,000 USD
* **2019 H1 Net Loss**: $905,000,000 USD

---

### NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES: "COMMUNITY ADJUSTED EBITDA"

To provide investors with additional insight into our location-level performance, we use a custom non-GAAP financial metric termed **"Community Adjusted EBITDA"**.

* **Community Adjusted EBITDA Definition**: Calculated as net loss before net interest expense, income tax, depreciation and amortization, adjusted for corporate general & administrative expenses ($389M in 2018), design and development costs, and sales and marketing expenses ($378M in 2018).
* **2018 Community Adjusted EBITDA**: Positive $467,000,000 USD.
* **Note on Limitation**: Management acknowledges that Community Adjusted EBITDA excludes significant overhead expenses necessary to operate the overall enterprise.

### RISK FACTORS & LEASE OBLIGATIONS

1. **Long-Term Lease Commitments vs. Short-Term Member Contracts**:
   - Total long-term undiscounted lease obligations: **$47,200,000,000 USD** over an average lease term of 15 years.
   - Average member contract commitment: 1 to 12 months.
   - Exposure: Economic downturns or drops in occupancy could severely impact ability to service long-term lease liabilities.

2. **Growth Capital Burn**:
   - Net cash used in operating activities: -$177 million (2018).
   - Capital expenditures for build-outs (CapEx): -$2.5 billion (2018).
