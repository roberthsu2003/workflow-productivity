# 我的英文寫作樣本（交換學生申請自我介紹初稿）

> 本文件為模擬教材，供 Claude Projects 課程練習使用。
> 文中刻意保留台灣學習者常見的英文錯誤，讓 AI 教練分析。

---

Dear Selection Committee,

My name is Kevin Lin and I am a junior student in National Taipei University of Business. I am very interesting in applying the exchange program to Osaka University because I think it is a very good chance for me.

Since I was a child, I like Japanese culture very much. I have studied Japanese for two years and I passed the JLPT N3 test on last December. I believe study in Japan can let my Japanese become more better and also open my international view.

In the school, I join the international volunteer club and be the leader of activity department. We hold many events to help foreign students adapt the life in Taiwan. This experience let me learn how to communicate with people from different culture.

If I have the honor to be selected, I will try my best to learn and make a good bridge between two schools. Thank you for your time to read my application.

Sincerely,
Kevin Lin
