# UNITED STATES SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION (SEC)
## FORM F-4 REGISTRATION STATEMENT - Gogoro Inc. (睿能創意) & Poema Global Holdings

**Filing Date**: November 18, 2021  
**Transaction Type**: Business Combination (SPAC Listing on NASDAQ: GGR)  
**Enterprise Valuation**: $2,350,000,000 USD ($2.35 Billion)  

---

### BUSINESS OVERVIEW & GHOSTATION NETWORK

Gogoro Inc. is a global leader in battery swapping ecosystems and urban mobility solutions. As of Q3 2021, Gogoro established a dense swapping network in Taiwan:
* **GoStation Network**: Over 2,100 GoStation locations across Taiwan.
* **Daily Swaps**: Over 370,000 daily battery swaps.
* **Subscribers**: Over 430,000 monthly battery swapping subscribers.

---

### CAPITAL EXPENDITURES (CAPEX) & FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS

1. **Battery Network Infrastructure Investment**:
   - Total cumulative CapEx for GoStation infrastructure & batteries: > $350 million USD.
   - Depreciation of battery packs: 8-year useful life amortized lineally.

2. **Revenue Breakdown**:
   - Hardware Sales (Smartscooters): ~ 65% of total revenue.
   - Battery Swapping Service Revenue (Recurring Monthly Subscriptions): ~ 35% of total revenue.

3. **Government Subsidies Context (Taiwan Market)**:
   - Consumer purchasing of Smartscooters is significantly influenced by Industrial Development Administration (IDA) & Environmental Protection Administration (EPA) subsidies in Taiwan, ranging from NT$7,000 to NT$10,000 per vehicle.
